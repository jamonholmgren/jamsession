# Jamsession Peasant Mob

Use for plans, research or diagnosis when the user requests Peasant Mob. It is
an experimental breadth-first workflow, not a proven replacement for premium
planning. Noble Court is the usual premium PPP, not this workflow.

Use `jamsession-summon-agent` for consent, scoped launch justification and
provider permission boundaries. Share task-relevant context only; exclude
secrets, credentials and unrelated private data. Carry any originating privacy
restrictions into every brief; delegation does not remove them.

or run parallel writers against the live checkout. Keep source inputs immutable.

## Draft, synthesize, circulate

1. Freeze one brief: objective, evidence, constraints, owned input paths, answer
   format and per-phase deadlines. Each lane must distinguish evidence from
   assumptions and identify its strongest objection. Launch eligible members
   concurrently in fresh sessions; do not expose other drafts in this phase.
2. Collect terminal outcomes once. Retain successful session IDs and name every
   failed/unavailable lane and reason. Leave failures behind throughout the run;
   continuing a successful session for feedback is not retrying a failed member.
3. Have a separate **Sol 6.1 LOW or Opus 5.5 LOW** orchestrator synthesize the
   drafts. Prefer its native harness; it is outside the eight-member roster.
   Record supported agreement, evidence-based dissent, rejected claims with
   reasons, and uncertainty. This is not majority voting or forced consensus.
4. **Feedback round 1:** give every surviving member the same candidate and
   evidence. Resume them concurrently for independent critiques, without showing
   one member's critique to another before collection. Synthesize all outcomes.
5. **Feedback round 2:** circulate that revised synthesis to every survivor,
   collect independent comments in parallel, then synthesize the final result.
   These are two feedback rounds AFTER initial drafts, not two total exchanges.
6. Third/fourth rounds are optional only for named unresolved material issues
   within the agreed budget/deadline. Stop at four; do not seek endless consensus.

A complete feedback round includes critiques from every surviving member and
an integrated synthesis. Name members dropped during each phase and report
reduced coverage; a pending or timed-out response is not a completed critique.
Do not claim the two-round minimum if the remaining panel cannot complete it.
If none survive, report the workflow incomplete rather than inventing a panel.

Return the final plan/analysis, supported minority objections and uncertainty,
completed feedback-round count, all eight lane outcomes, actual providers,
models, efforts and session IDs. Report exclusions and pricing uncertainty.
Do not claim Peasant Mob beats Sol, Opus or Noble Court without measured results;
any separate evaluation owns its fixtures, trials and conclusions. Send only
substantive findings, blockers, agreed checkpoints and final handoffs, not chatter.
